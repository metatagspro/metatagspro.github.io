Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Meta Tags Pro (“we”, “us”) is an independent software product operated from India. It is a Chrome extension built so that your pages, your audit results and your browsing stay on your computer. This page explains exactly what that means. Questions go to abhij89@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We run no server that receives your pages, audit results or browsing history.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking in the extension or on this website.
  • Pro checks your license key with Gumroad. That is the only data the extension sends to a service we choose.

What the extension can access

The extension asks Chrome for three permissions when you install it: activeTab and scripting, so the page check can read the tab you are looking at when you click the extension, and storage, so it can remember settings on your computer.

It does not request access to all websites at install time. Access to a site for the whole-site audit is optional. Chrome shows a prompt for your site (both http and https, with and without www) when you start an audit, and only if you approve does the crawl begin. You can withdraw that access at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

What is stored, and where

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it. You can also delete any saved audit inside the extension.

What the crawl fetches

When you audit a site, your browser requests that site's pages, its robots.txt file and its sitemap, the same requests a visitor or a search engine makes. They go from your browser straight to the site you chose. The results are analysed in your browser and are not copied anywhere else.

The one-click page check reads the tab you click it on. To check for a robots.txt and a sitemap it requests those two files from the same site.

Links to other tools

The page check includes links to third-party tools such as validators and domain reports. Nothing is sent until you click one. When you do, the address or domain of the page you are on is part of the link, and the tool's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Buying and the license check

Payment is handled by Gumroad. You pay on Gumroad's page, and Gumroad collects your payment details and email address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. Gumroad tells us about your purchase, including your email address, so we can support you and process refunds.

When you paste a license key into the extension, the extension sends one request to api.gumroad.com with the key and our product identifier, to confirm that the key is valid, belongs to this product and has not been refunded, charged back or ended. The extension repeats the check at most once a day while it is in use. Gumroad receives the key and your connection details (such as your IP address) as it does for any request, and Gumroad returns whether the license is active. We do not receive a copy of that request.

The extension asks Chrome for access to api.gumroad.com only when you click to activate a license.

A license can be activated on up to 3 browsers. The count is kept by Gumroad. Removing the license inside the extension clears it from that browser only. It does not release the activation, so write to us if you need an activation reset.

This website

This website is plain pages with no scripts, cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children

Meta Tags Pro is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be mentioned in the extension's release notes on the Chrome Web Store.
