Refund policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

If Meta Tags Pro is not right for you, you can have your money back within 14 days of purchase. No questions asked, and no need to explain.

How to ask

  1. Email abhij89@gmail.com from the address you bought with, or include the order number from your Gumroad receipt.
  2. We refund the full amount through Gumroad. This usually happens within one working day, and your bank may take a few more days to show it.

What happens to your license

A refunded license stops unlocking Pro. The extension notices at its next license check and returns to the free plan. Your saved audits stay on your computer and the free features keep working.

Renewals

Pro renews yearly until you cancel. You can cancel from the link in your Gumroad receipt. If a renewal charge surprises you, write to us within 14 days of that charge and we will refund it on the same terms.

Problems instead of a refund

If something is not working, tell us first. We would like to fix it. A refund is still yours if you prefer it.
